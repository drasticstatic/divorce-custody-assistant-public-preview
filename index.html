Divorce & Custody Assistant

Custody · 23 Pa.C.S. § 5328 · the law is the frame

The children’s best interest is two loving parents in their lives and a secure foundation to build a family upon — even after divorce.

The controlling statute was rewritten by Act 8 of 2024 and Act 11 of 2025 (effective ~Aug 2025). Safety is now paramount: the court must give substantial weighted consideration to the safety of the child. And the factor this father pursued from the outset — which party is more likely to encourage & permit frequent and continuing contact — once “Factor (1),” is now (2.3)(i), with a tail attached: contact is encouraged “if consistent with the safety needs of the child.” The contest over access now runs through safety, not around it.

⭐ #1 priority

Factor (2.3)(i) — frequent & continuing contact if consistent with the child’s safety

From the outset, the father has sought shared & equal custodial access (50/50) and holds that the children shall always have equal access to both loving parents. A verifiable record of denied and disrupted contact exists and is held with the court — offered as proof, not presumption, that contact was not the father’s gate to close. The statutory qualifier is acknowledged: the father’s position is that equal access is consistent with the children’s safety.

🛡️ The safety cluster carries substantial weighted consideration: (1) which party is more likely to ensure the child’s safety; (2) present/past abuse where there has been a finding; (2.1) protective-services involvement; (2.2) violent or assaultive behavior.

A fuller, factor-by-factor companion — the old→new factor map and the father’s public-safe position on each — was filed with the Court. The detailed filed document is scrubbed from this public preview: this surface holds the structure and posture, not protected detail.
