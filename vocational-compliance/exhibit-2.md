This exhibit is an attorney briefing memo establishing **Trader Tax Status
(TTS)** and a genuine **business profit motive** &mdash; the legal framework
needed to defeat "willful underemployment" allegations and any attempt to
characterize the quantitative trading and developer work as a pretext for
hiding income or avoiding support obligations. Below is the public spine of
the argument; the case-specific application lives in the filed version.

## The question this memorandum answers

When a regulated-trading and software-development operation is also the
response to a work-search order, the ordinary custody-support framework can
misread it. Two mischaracterizations are the risk:

1. **"Willful underemployment"** &mdash; the claim that the filer is earning
   below capacity by choice, in order to suppress the support calculation.
2. **"Speculative hobby"** &mdash; the claim that the trading and development
   is recreation rather than a real business, and therefore its losses and
   hours should not count.

The memo answers both with the same evidentiary record the rest of the packet
relies on: the live public commit history and the proprietary-firm evaluation
results documented in [Exhibit 4](exhibit-4-log.md).

## The controlling standard

The memorandum establishes that, under **Internal Revenue Code &sect; 183 (the
Hobby Loss Rules)** and the federal regulatory standards governing Trader Tax
Status, the quantitative-futures-trading and Web3-software-development
operations constitute a legitimate, structured, pre-revenue startup business
venture &mdash; not a "hobby." Under Pennsylvania family-law jurisprudence, a
temporary lack of immediate net income during a business-launch and intensive
vocational-retraining phase does **not** constitute willful underemployment or
an evasion of support obligations when executed in good faith to maximize
long-term earning capacity. A child-support obligation is calculated on earning
capacity; a temporary reduction in income during a career transition does not
equal willful underemployment if the pivot is executed in good faith.

## The framework (public spine)

The argument rests on the established requirements for trader tax status and a
bona-fide business profit motive, *applied rather than merely asserted*:

- **Trader Tax Status** &mdash; the operation is carried on with continuity
  and regularity, the activity is substantial, and it is pursued with the
  intent to profit from short-term market swings rather than as a casual or
  interest-seeking activity. The substantiality and continuity are what the
  live session schedule (daily market sessions across multiple programs,
  weekday terminal presence aligned to the global liquidity cycle) and the
  prop-firm combine record supply in evidence.

- **Business profit motive &mdash; the §183 factors, applied.** The activity
  is conducted in a businesslike manner (structured curricula, daily and
  weekly live calls, risk-parameter auditing), depends on the skill and effort
  of the taxpayer (mentor-led retraining, one-on-one audits, verified
  technical progression), and is entered into with the actual and honest
  objective of making a profit. The taxpayer's time and effort, the expertise
  brought to bear, the history of income or losses in appreciating-asset
  activities, and the substantial business investment in equipment, education,
  and software all weigh toward a genuine profit motive rather than
  recreation. The public engineering labor &mdash; maintained repositories,
  deployments, recruitment-safeguards research &mdash; is itself evidence of a
  career-scale effort, not a sideline.

- **Substantial business investment.** Meaningful financial and structural
  commitments have been made to professional, high-tier quantitative mentorships
  and software. These operational agreements are the defendant's sole business
  liabilities *by contract* &mdash; capital investments to restore earning
  capacity, not discretionary spending.

- **Defeating the underemployment characterization.** A pivot that
  *maximizes long-range earning capacity* is the opposite of voluntary idleness.
  A court that credits the documented training intensity and the verifiable
  nature of the modern-sector placement channels (GitHub commits, protocol
  deployments, prop-firm combines) cannot fairly call the result
  "underemployment" in the statute's sense. The transition was *involuntary*
  and the response has been to *raise* capacity, not lower it to suppress a
  calculation.

- **The asset character is not concealed cash.** Prop-firm evaluation and
  verification accounts are performance-based simulation protocols operated
  under power-of-attorney-style arrangements aimed at securing independent
  (1099) income; they hold no live marital cash value and have produced no
  realized payout to date. The rules, capital access, and payout frameworks of
  these entities are volatile and subject to arbitrary corporate revision. The
  unedited trading methodology and metrics are publicly hosted and preserved in
  the live trade journal on GitHub &mdash; in tandem with third-party trade
  analytics &mdash; precisely so the record is open to inspection.
