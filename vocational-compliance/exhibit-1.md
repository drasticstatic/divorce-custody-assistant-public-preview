## III. Vocational retraining & the reality of a 40+ hour weekly labor

Following an involuntary separation from a historical computer-numerically
controlled (CNC) programming and precision-manufacturing career, the filer
executed an intensive, mentor-led transition into advanced digital engineering
as a full-stack Web3 / blockchain software developer and quantitative futures
trader. The separation was not a voluntary resignation designed to evade
support; it resulted from systemic operational conflicts, and the response to
it has been to *maximize* long-range earning capacity rather than settle for a
dead-end wage floor.

The work runs 40 to 80 hours per week. A baseline terminal presence of
roughly 08:00 to 21:00 Eastern aligns directly with the global traditional
market liquidity cycles &mdash; the equity market close, the futures
cool-down, and the decentralized protocol settlement reset &mdash; so the
trading and development labor stay synchronized with the markets they
operate in. Proprietary futures evaluation challenges run on a desktop
terminal while live open- and closed-source repositories, scripts, bots, and
cross-platform agents are maintained and calibrated alongside.

Prior to the formal transition in employment status, the filer had already
dedicated off-hours to self-funded curricula to formalize credentials in
systems engineering and proprietary trading. The retraining is **not a hobby
or passive endeavor** &mdash; it is a highly specialized, technical pivot
designed to supplement and maximize long-term earning capacity. It is
delivered through bespoke learning-management platforms and enterprise
collaboration surfaces, verified by elite technical frameworks, and operates
under strict continuous daily commitment and weekly live-session
constraints. The active programs, each with a public, verifiable footprint,
include:

- **Dapp University** &mdash; specialized blockchain developer mentorship and
  mastery programs for full-stack application architecture and smart-contract
  design & deployment. Live group class sessions run weekday mornings,
  supplemented by weekly one-on-one developer audits and monthly masterclasses,
  with long-term colleagues and a support cohort.

- **Inevitrade Premium (Lifetime) + Inner Circle** &mdash; smart-money
  concepts, quantitative market-structure coaching, technical analysis, and
  algorithmic risk management. Live daily market sessions run weekdays,
  supplemented by a weekly live mentoring session.

- **ZeroToHero** &mdash; proprietary futures trading execution, prop-firm
  evaluation coaching, and risk-parameter auditing. Live daily market sessions
  and live daily mastermind classes run six days a week, supplemented by
  on-demand weekly one-on-one coaching audits and weekly shared group audits.

- **SmartTradingBlueprint** &mdash; advanced quantitative trading architecture,
  ICT market-concept coaching, and expanding into live London market sessions.
  Live daily market sessions bridge the London and New York opens, supplemented
  by a weekly live mentoring session and bi-weekly group psychology sessions.

Delivery surfaces include standard enterprise collaboration and learning
platforms (video conferencing, community chat, course platforms, and creator

## IV. Redefining "good-faith work search" for the modern tech sector

A Work Search Order required a set number of job contacts bi-weekly. While
respecting the compliance intent of that order, forcing this labor into an
entry-level, non-technical role via standard job boards could result in a
dead-end, low-wage outcome that would permanently devalue long-term support
capacity and ignore the structural reality of the software-engineering market.

In the decentralized Web3 engineering sector, traditional corporate resumes are
effectively obsolete. Technical competencies, independent-contractor (1099)
payouts, and DAO developer grants are secured through **verified GitHub
commits, live protocol deployments, open-source contributions, and corporate
proprietary trading evaluation combines** (e.g., recognizable prop-firm
funding programs). To satisfy the legal obligations in absolute good faith, the
filer compiled **Exhibit 4 &mdash; the Vocational Activity & Tech Work-Search
Log**, which translates software commits, developer outreach, and proprietary
risk-management metrics into quantifiable compliance equivalents across the
reporting window. The request is that the vocational training and business
startup phase be recognized as an active, legitimate job-search program and
capacity-restoration initiative.
