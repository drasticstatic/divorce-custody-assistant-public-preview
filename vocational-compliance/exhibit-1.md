## IV. Redefining "good-faith work search" for the modern tech sector

A Work Search Order required a set number of job contacts bi-weekly. While
respecting the compliance intent of that order, forcing this labor into an
entry-level, non-technical role via standard job boards could result in a
dead-end, low-wage outcome that would permanently devalue long-term support
capacity and ignore the structural reality of the software-engineering market.

In the decentralized Web3 engineering sector, traditional corporate resumes are
effectively obsolete. Technical competencies, independent-contractor payouts,
and DAO developer grants are secured through **verified GitHub commits, live
protocol deployments, open-source contributions, and corporate proprietary
trading evaluation combines** (e.g., Apex Trader Funding, Take Profit Trader).

To satisfy the legal obligations in absolute good faith, the filer compiled
**Exhibit 4 &mdash; the Vocational Activity & Tech Work-Search Log**, which
translates software commits, developer outreach, and proprietary
risk-management metrics into quantifiable compliance equivalents. The request
is that the vocational training and business startup phase be recognized as
an active, legitimate job-search program and capacity-restoration
initiative.
